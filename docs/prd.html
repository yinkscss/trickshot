01 Executive Summary

Trick Shot is a mobile-first, single-input arcade game — a precision “swing the ball through a chain of hoops” loop in the spirit of Ketchapp-style drop/swing games — built on Celo, an Ethereum L2 optimized for low-cost, mobile-friendly stablecoin payments.

Unlike cosmetic-and-boast models, Trick Shot’s monetization is built around three levers: powerup purchases, pay-to-continue, and a season pass — layered on skill-based tournaments where the house takes a 15% rake of the prize pool.

Celo remains the settlement layer: sub-cent fees, gas payable in cUSD/USDC/USDT, ~1s blocks, and distribution via Opera’s MiniPay wallet (16M+ wallets across 65+ countries).

02 Problem & Opportunity

Problem

Endless-chain arcade games (Dunk Shot, Helix Jump, Stack) monetize almost entirely through interstitial ads and app-store IAP for continues. That caps margins (15–30% store take), makes revenge-spend opaque, and leaves no verifiable competitive layer the studio owns.

Opportunity

  • Sell powerups & continues in stablecoins at near-zero gas — a $0.49 continue doesn’t lose 30¢ to processors.
  • Run transparent on-chain tournaments with a fixed, visible house rake.
  • Use MiniPay as a low-CAC channel where stablecoin rails beat cards.

03 Goals & Success Metrics

Business Goals

  1. F2P core loop with paid powerups, continues, and seasonal subscription — positive unit economics within 2 quarters.
  2. Skill-based tournaments with 15% house rake as recurring revenue.
  3. Day-30 retention ≥ 20% and paying-user rate ≥ 6%, with continues as the primary conversion moment.

Non-Goals (v1)

  • No peer-to-peer wagering pools — fixed entry fee, pooled prize, house rake only.
  • No speculative token or yield product at launch.
  • No randomized paid loot boxes — fixed-price, fixed-effect purchases only.

05 Core Gameplay

Hold hoop → Drag aim → Release → Swish / star → Next hoop → Chain++

5.1 Core Loop (v1)

  • Single-input swing: drag back to aim, release to launch from the current hoop into the next.
  • Chain scoring: consecutive clean swings build a multiplier; a miss ends the run unless continue/shield.
  • Obstacles: spinner bars, moving hoops, narrow-gap hoops that raise the skill ceiling.
  • Stars: pickups along the chain feed soft currency (cosmetics & minor powerups without cUSD).
  • Daily challenges, streaks, local/global leaderboards.

5.2 Web3-Native Features

  • Invisible wallet via Magic.link — email/social/phone, non-custodial behind the scenes.
  • On-chain run attestation: verified best chain minted as optional shareable proof.
  • Tournaments: cUSD entry, contract-escrowed prize pool, automated payouts; house takes 15% rake, verifiable on-chain before entry.

Why on-chain rails win

  • Micro-tx viability: $0.49 continues clear at almost any volume with sub-cent gas.
  • Transparent rake: prize pool + 15% cut settle via public contract — auditable trust.
  • Fast settlement: ~1s blocks keep continue purchases inside flow state.
  • Store fee avoidance: web/PWA checkout where policy allows keeps 15–30% cut off high-frequency buys.
Guardrails: Powerups banned in Tournament mode. Continues allowed but score-penalized. Daily spend caps + self-exclusion. No RNG purchases. Legal review per jurisdiction required before real-money tournaments — this PRD is not legal advice.
